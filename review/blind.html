Deming blind labelling

Label each device yourself from its name and firmware. All devices are synthetic. Do this before you open the label review page, and don't look at any answers while you work.

Lanes

  • patch: the product is supported, it has a known exploited vulnerability (CISA KEV) at this version, and the vendor has a fix.
  • retire: the vendor no longer supports it (past end of life or end of security support), even if it is also in KEV.
  • fine: supported, and no known exploited vulnerability at this version.
  • unknown: you can't tell what it is, or can't decide from the name and firmware. Never guess.

Use public sources only: the vendor's own security advisory or end-of-life page, and the CISA KEV catalog. Judge support dates as of 2026-11-12.
